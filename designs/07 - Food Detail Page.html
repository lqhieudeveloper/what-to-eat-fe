About
Phở Tái is a traditional Vietnamese soup made with aromatic beef broth, fresh rice noodles, and thin slices of rare beef that cook in the hot broth. It's a beloved comfort food that's light yet satisfying, perfect for any time of day. The complex broth flavor comes from slow-cooked beef bones, star anise, cinnamon, and other spices.
